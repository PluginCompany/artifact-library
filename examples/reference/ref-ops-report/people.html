Saturdays are the constraint

Hours scheduled, worked and spent with a bike on the stand, utilisation by shift, and whether every shift has enough qualified mechanics.

Sample data

Hours per week

Absence peaked in early August. Hours on the stand climbed in September as utilisation improved. The axis starts at 200 h.

Utilisation by shift

Every shift improved each month, but Saturday is still 10 to 12 points behind the opening shift.

Absence by reason

Summer holiday accounts for half of all absence.

Skills coverage

Qualified mechanics rostered per shift. On Saturdays a single mechanic covers suspension, wheel builds and e-bike motors.

RecommendationCross-train two Saturday mechanics on suspension service and e-bike motors in Q4, so that one absence no longer turns those bikes away.
