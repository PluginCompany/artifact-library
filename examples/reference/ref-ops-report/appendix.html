Methodology

Weekly figures run Monday to Sunday (ISO weeks W27–W39). A week counts towards the month its Monday falls in, so July has five weeks and August and September four each.

Repairs and bikes come from repair tickets: a repair counts when the mechanic closes it on the workshop board, and a bike counts when it is checked out to its owner. Plans are the weekly targets on the board, set from the booking calendar. Comebacks are bikes returned within 30 days and logged against the original ticket.

Hours come from the staff rota (scheduled), absence records (absence) and repair tickets (time on the stand). Changes are against Q2 2026, which used the same method.

Data noteW34 includes a public holiday. Plans and scheduled hours for that week cover four days, so the dip in W34 is expected.

Definitions

Plan attainment
Repairs completed ÷ repairs planned.
First-time fix
Share of repairs that did not come back within 30 days.
Comeback
A bike returned within 30 days with a problem from its repair. Weekly comebacks = repairs × (1 − first-time-fix rate).
Ready on time
Bikes ready by the promised pick-up time ÷ bikes checked out.
Bike at risk
A bike in the workshop whose forecast finish is after its promised pick-up time, as flagged on the board.
Utilisation
Hours with a bike on the stand ÷ hours worked.
Skill gap
A shift and skill with fewer qualified mechanics than the target of 2.
